Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about admissions, campus life, and more.

Yes, we provide separate hostel facilities for boys and girls on campus. The hostels are equipped with modern amenities including Wi-Fi, mess facilities, 24/7 security, and recreational areas.
Our dedicated placement cell works with 100+ companies including TCS, Infosys, Wipro, and more. We have a 95% placement rate with average packages ranging from ₹4-8 LPA for undergraduate students.
Yes, we offer merit-based scholarships, need-based financial aid, and sports quotas. Scholarships range from 25% to 100% of tuition fees based on academic performance and financial background.
We offer a wide range of activities including sports, cultural clubs, NSS, NCC, tech clubs, literary societies, and annual fests. Students can join any number of clubs and participate in inter-college competitions.
