Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about admissions, campus life, and more.

FYJC admissions follow the Maharashtra Government CAP (Centralized Admission Process) rounds. Students must apply online through the government portal. St. Raphael's College also conducts minority and management quota admissions. Documents required include SSC marksheet, transfer certificate, caste certificate (if applicable), and photographs. Check the college website for the latest admission schedule.
We offer BA (English, Economics, Psychology, Sociology, History), B.Com., B.Com. (MS), B.Sc. IT, BAF, BMS, BAMMC, BBI, B.Sc. (Hospitality Studies), and M.Com. programs. We have both Aided and Self-Financed (SFC) courses under the University of Mumbai.
St. Raphael's College has been re-accredited with an 'A grade and CGPA of 3.25 in the 3rd cycle of NAAC accreditation (2023). We have been accredited since 2004 and consistently maintain high academic standards.
The current Principal is Prof. (Dr.) Meera Deshpande, who has been serving since 2024. She is assisted by Dr. Rajesh Kulkarni (Vice Principal – Aided), Dr. Priya Sharma (Vice Principal – Self-Financed), and Mr. Vikram Patel (Vice Principal – Junior College).
