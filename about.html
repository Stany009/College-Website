About St. Andrew's College

Five decades of academic excellence, innovation, and community building. Discover our journey from a small institution to a premier educational establishment.

A Legacy of Excellence Since 1976

St. Andrew's College of Arts, Science and Commerce, located in the heart of Bandra (West), Mumbai, is a premier institution of higher education affiliated to the University of Mumbai. The college has been a beacon of academic excellence, offering diverse undergraduate and postgraduate programs.

Re-accredited with an 'A+' grade and CGPA of 3.35 in the 4th cycle of NAAC accreditation, the college has consistently been recognized for its academic rigor, research output, and holistic student development. Our management, led by Archbishop John Rodrigues (Chairman, Board of Trustees), is committed to the Jesuit tradition of "Education for All."

Vision & Mission

🔭

Our Vision

To be a premier institution of higher education that fosters academic excellence, research, and holistic development, producing socially responsible and morally upright citizens who contribute meaningfully to society.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide accessible, inclusive, and quality education through innovative teaching, research, and community engagement. We are committed to the Jesuit tradition of forming men and women for others, nurturing critical thinking, and building character to prepare students for the challenges of tomorrow.

Principal's Message

"At St. Andrew's College, we are committed to providing quality education that transforms lives. Our mission is to nurture academically excellent, socially responsible, and morally upright citizens who will contribute meaningfully to society."

Milestones Through the Years

76

1976 – Foundation

St. Andrew's College established with 3 courses and 120 students in a single building.

85

1985 – Expansion

New campus building inaugurated. Science and Commerce departments added.

95

1995 – Digital Leap

Computer lab established. IT department launched. First batch of 200+ graduates.

06

2006 – NAAC Accreditation

Awarded 'A' grade by NAAC. Library digitized. Sports complex inaugurated.

15

2015 – Smart Campus

Smart classrooms introduced. Wi-Fi campus. Student portal launched.

26

2026 – Today

5,000+ students, 50+ courses, 95% placement rate, and a thriving alumni network of 50,000+.

Research & Publications

Driving innovation through student and faculty research initiatives.

Andrean Research Journal & Cinemania

We publish the renowned Andrean Research Journal and The Cinemania, showcasing academic excellence and creative insights from our students and faculty.

Ethics & Society / Ruminations

A dedicated platform for deep philosophical and societal discussions. Ruminations is our annual publication documenting these thought-provoking seminars.
