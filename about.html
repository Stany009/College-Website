About St. Andrew's College

Five decades of academic excellence, innovation, and community building. Discover our journey from a small institution to a premier educational establishment.

A Legacy of Excellence Since 1976

Founded in 1976, St. Andrew's College began with a vision to provide quality education that is accessible to all. What started as a small institution with just 3 courses and 120 students has grown into a premier educational establishment offering 50+ courses to over 5,000 students.

Our founders believed that education should not just fill minds but transform lives. That philosophy continues to guide us today as we blend traditional academic values with modern teaching methodologies and state-of-the-art infrastructure.

Vision & Mission

🔭

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized center of academic excellence that empowers students to become innovative leaders, critical thinkers, and responsible global citizens who contribute meaningfully to society.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide accessible, inclusive, and transformative education through innovative pedagogy, industry partnerships, and holistic development. We nurture curiosity, foster research, and build character to prepare students for the challenges of tomorrow.

Principal's Message

"Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world. At St. Andrew's, we don't just teach subjects—we shape futures. Our commitment is to provide an environment where every student discovers their potential, develops critical thinking, and emerges as a confident, compassionate leader ready to make a difference."
Dr. Rajesh Kumar

Principal, St. Andrew's College • Ph.D. in Education, Cambridge University

Milestones Through the Years

1976 – Foundation

85
