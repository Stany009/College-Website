Admissions

Admission Procedure

  1. Fill out the online application form.
  2. Submit required documents.
  3. Appear for the entrance test/interview (if applicable).
  4. Pay the admission fees upon selection.

Eligibility

A minimum of 50% in the qualifying examination is required for undergraduate courses.

Documents Required

  • 10th and 12th Marksheets
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Passport Size Photographs
  • ID Proof

Fee Structure

Please contact the admission office for detailed fee structures for the current academic year.

FAQs

Is there a hostel facility available?

Yes, separate hostels for boys and girls are available on campus.
