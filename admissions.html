Eligibility & Documents

📋 Eligibility Criteria

  • ✓ Minimum 50% in qualifying examination (10+2) for UG courses
  • ✓ Minimum 55% in graduation for PG courses
  • ✓ Valid entrance test score (where applicable)
  • ✓ Age limit: 17-25 years for UG courses

📁 Documents Required

  • 📄 10th and 12th Marksheets (Original + Copies)
  • 📄 Transfer Certificate (TC)
  • 📷 Passport Size Photographs (6 copies)
  • 🪪 Valid ID Proof (Aadhaar/Passport)
  • 📄 Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • 📄 Migration Certificate
